01 / ManoMano / 2026

Growing cross-border sellers on a declining platform

Owned the full commercial cycle for international sellers across 5 European markets, from outreach and negotiation to contract expansion. The portfolio kept growing while the platform overall declined.

Commercial negotiation

Ran the negotiation cycle end to end: first outreach, commercial terms, signature, then renewal and expansion. Beyond onboarding, this meant negotiating category-specific promotional placements with sellers, flash sales, added visibility, and summer peak campaigns, and making the case for why a given seller should commit stock and pricing to a market they had not prioritised.

Portfolio analysis

Went through the portfolio market by market, comparing what each seller listed against what was actually selling in the other four countries. Where a catalogue gap lined up with real cross-border demand, that became a concrete expansion pitch. This surfaced 20+ opportunities and decided which sellers to push into which markets first.

Ads diagnostics

Audited seller advertising performance and translated it into plain recommendations: which campaigns were spending without return, which categories deserved more budget, which listings were being held back by weak content rather than weak bids. Sellers acted on the summary without having to read the dashboards themselves.
